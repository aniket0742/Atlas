# Billing and Refunds

## Refund window

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of the original purchase
date. Refunds are returned to the original payment method and typically settle
within 5 to 10 business days.

Refunds requested after 30 days are declined automatically. Support agents
cannot override this window; exceptions require written approval from the
finance team.

## Annual plans

Annual plans are refunded on a prorated basis from the cancellation date. The
unused portion is calculated in whole months, not days.

## Chargebacks

If a customer initiates a chargeback rather than requesting a refund, the
account is suspended immediately and the case is routed to the finance team.
Accounts are reinstated once the chargeback is withdrawn or resolved.

## Failed payments

After a failed payment, the system retries on days 1, 3 and 7. The account moves
to a grace period for 14 days, after which it is downgraded to the free tier.
Data is retained for 90 days after downgrade.
