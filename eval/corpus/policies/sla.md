# Service Level Agreement

## Availability commitment

The platform targets 99.9% monthly uptime for Standard contracts and 99.95% for
Enterprise contracts. Uptime is measured as successful responses to the health
endpoint, sampled once per minute from three regions.

## Exclusions

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance does not count
against the availability commitment. Nor does downtime caused by the customer's
own misconfiguration, or by a third-party integration the customer has enabled.

## Credit schedule

| Monthly uptime | Credit |
|---|---|
| below 99.9% | 10% of monthly fee |
| below 99.0% | 25% of monthly fee |
| below 95.0% | 50% of monthly fee |

Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month.
Unclaimed credits are not applied retroactively.

## Support response

SLA response targets are covered separately in the support policy. Availability
credits and support response targets are independent commitments.
