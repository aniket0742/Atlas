# Acceptable Use

## Prohibited content

Customers may not upload content they do not have the right to process, content
that is unlawful in the jurisdiction where it is stored, or content whose
processing would require a licence the customer does not hold.

## Automated access

API access is permitted and encouraged. Scraping the web console with headless
browsers is not, because it bypasses the rate limiting applied at the API layer
and destabilises shared infrastructure.

## Resource limits

A single tenant may not sustain more than 40% of a shared cluster's capacity.
Customers approaching that share are contacted and offered a dedicated cluster
before any enforcement action is taken.

## Enforcement

The first response to a violation is always contact, not suspension. Accounts
are suspended without prior contact only where continued operation presents an
immediate security or legal risk.
