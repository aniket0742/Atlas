# Support Policy

## Response targets

Support responds to Standard tier tickets within one business day. Priority tier
tickets receive a response within four business hours. These are response
targets, not resolution targets.

## Escalation

A ticket is escalated to engineering when it is reproducible, affects more than
one customer, and has no documented workaround. Escalated tickets are reviewed
at the daily triage meeting.

## Out of scope

Support does not write custom integration code on a customer's behalf, and does
not provide advice on the customer's own regulatory compliance obligations.
