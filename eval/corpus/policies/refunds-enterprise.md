# Enterprise Refunds and Credits

Enterprise contracts are governed by the signed order form, not by the
self-serve billing policy. Where the two disagree, the order form wins.

## Refund window

Enterprise customers may request a refund within 90 days of an invoice date,
not the 30 days that applies to self-serve plans. Requests after 90 days are
handled as service credits against the next invoice rather than as refunds.

## Approval

Refunds above 10,000 USD require sign-off from both the account executive and
the finance director. Below that threshold the account executive may approve
alone.

## Service credits

Credits are applied to the next invoice and do not expire while the contract is
active. Unused credits are forfeited at contract termination and are never paid
out in cash.

## Mid-term downgrades

Reducing seat count mid-term does not generate a refund. The lower seat count
takes effect at the next renewal date.
