# Data Retention

## Customer data

Active customer data is retained for the life of the account. After account
closure, data is retained for 90 days and then deleted.

## Logs

Application logs are retained for 30 days. Audit logs are retained for 400 days
because several customer contracts require at least one year.

## Backups

Database backups are taken nightly and retained for 35 days. Backups are
encrypted at rest and restore is tested quarterly.
