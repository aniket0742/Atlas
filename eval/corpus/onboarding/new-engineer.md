# New Engineer Onboarding

## First day

Your manager creates the accounts checklist before you start. If anything is
missing, raise it in the onboarding channel rather than waiting; access requests
take a day and the delay compounds.

Hardware is issued on the first day. Requests go to IT through the helpdesk.

## First week

The goal is one merged change, however small. Fixing a typo in a runbook counts.
The point is to exercise the whole path from clone to production before you need
it under pressure.

## Reading order

1. The architecture overview
2. The search architecture document
3. The deployment and migration documents
4. The incident response runbook

Read the incident response runbook before your first on-call shadow, not during.

## Shadowing

New engineers shadow on-call for two full rotations before joining the rotation
itself. Shadowing means receiving the pages and discussing the response, not
handling them alone.

## Mentor

Every new engineer is assigned a mentor for their first 90 days. The mentor is
deliberately not their manager, so that asking a naive question carries no
performance implication.
